The big market drop

"When the market is on sale"

The idea

Big market crashes are rare and scary, and most people sell. Instead of guessing the bottom, the strategy waits for the first bounce and then signals buys in small steps while prices are still low.

IT SIGNALS WHEN

01
The market fell hard: the S&P 500 is 20% or more below its high.
02
The first bounce: it climbs 5% off its low. That's the first step.
03
Then in steps: one more step every 2 weeks, until the budget is used.
