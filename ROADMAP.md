# Roadmap

The backlog for Strategy Trading Lab. Agents take work from **Next**, in order, one item per PR, and move it to **Done** with its PR number when it merges. Ernest sets the order; agents may add items, ideas and open decisions, and say so in their PR.

Screens and behaviour are in the [spec](docs/strategy-trading-lab-app-spec.md). Ernest's own notes are in [TODO.md](TODO.md). Each item in Next has its kind of change (see `CLAUDE.md`) and where the spec stands on it:

- *Spec: drawn* or *designed*: build it from the spec.
- *Spec: gap*: proposed here. Write the spec section in the same PR.

## The launch product

The goal of Next is a **minimum coherent product** that Ernest can publish and run on his own. That means:

- **A stranger can use it end to end.** They look around without an account, sign up with an invite, start a strategy in either mode from any template on offer, add their own stocks and change the list later, get their emails, confirm live signals, fix a wrong trade, archive a strategy, and export or delete their data. Nothing on screen leads to a dead end or to something that never runs: what isn't built is hidden.
- **Ernest can run it.** He sees whether the daily job ran, gets an email when it fails, sees who signed up, decides who can sign up, blocks an account, and gets feedback from users. Limits stop one user from loading the server. New stocks need no deploy.
- **It can be published.** It has legal pages, its own domain and email sender, an unsubscribe link, honest backtest figures, and a security pass behind it.

What it leaves out, so it ships sooner (still an open decision, at the bottom; *Your AI's top picks* is built and can come in):

| At launch | After launch |
| --- | --- |
| *Winners at a discount*, *The comeback*, *Index trend*, *Bear markets*, and *Your AI's top picks* (built, #70; in the launch unless Ernest leaves it out) | The AI comparison page |
| The starter lists, "+ Add a stock", the fund picker, Edit list on a running strategy | |
| Alerts by email | Web push |
| Tested results of the defaults, honestly labelled | Re-running the backtest with your settings, the Backtest page (its tile hidden until then) |
| Rename, archive, go live, alerts switch, trades you can fix, Tune the settings on a running strategy, the stock page | Change the budget |

The path, after the regulation note's *Path by stage* ([docs/regulation-spain-eu.md](docs/regulation-spain-eu.md)): **Milestone 1** finishes the core loop, **Milestone 2** makes it publishable, then a **quiet launch** (invite only from then on; sign-up stays open while the app is built), then **Milestone 3**, the **public beta**.

## After launch

Roughly in the order they become useful:

- **AI comparison page**: results by AI model from the shared rounds (never a ranking of stocks); needs a fresh legal look before it shows anything across users (regulation note, section 6).
- **Web push** alerts, with the iPhone home-screen step; then Choose the mode gets its notification copy back.
- **Change a running strategy**: change the budget, Tune the settings (Edit list is item 15).
- **Stock page**: chart with the zone shaded, status history, trades.
- **Backtest engine**: re-run with the user's settings and stocks (Advanced settings' "Yours" column, and each strategy's own past result), the Backtest page, and Advanced settings for the other templates.
- Template texts and backtest figures in the user's currency.
- Daily overview settings (a time of your choice).
- AI comparison page (results by AI model, never a ranking of stocks).
- Store a daily result per strategy, once strategies get long or many (today results are worked out on every request, ended and archived ones too, though theirs are frozen: #91 review).
- **Set your budget, with the ticked stocks** (low priority). "Enough for 6 stocks bought in full" ignores how many stocks are ticked, and can say "enough for 0 stocks" when a step is larger than a stock's maximum.
- **Set your budget, out-of-range values** (low priority). A typed value outside the limits is changed without a word. Say what changed and why, next to the field.
- **AI rounds, lighter reads** (low priority). `ChangesAsync` runs one price query per round session on every strategy page load; the dashboard GET sweeps each AI strategy with its own query (loading full answers); the round history returns every prompt and answer in one payload; `aiRequest` repeats `tradesRequest`. Load closes once, sweep in one query, fetch a round's text when it opens, share one request helper.
