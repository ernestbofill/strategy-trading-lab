Your AI's top picks

"Let your AI do the reading"

The idea

Nobody can read every news story and financial report about every company, but an AI can. You run our prompt in the AI you already use; it scores each stock, and the strategy signals the top one. You see whether all that reading turns into better picks.

It signals when

01
Every 2 weeks you run the AI round: copy the prompt, paste it into your AI, paste its answer back.
02
The stock with the top score gets a signal for one step, until the budget is used.
03
If you skip a round, there's no signal that round.

What the AI reads

At least one stays on. Each one adds a section to the prompt.

The prompt

Edit prompt
// suggested prompt · v1
Today is [date]. Search the web for news and data from the last 2 weeks, plus each company's latest quarterly figures.
Score each stock below from 0 to 100 on:
· News: [criteria]
· Mood: [criteria]
· Figures: [criteria]
and overall. Give a one-line reason and your main sources, with dates.
Answer only in this table: | Stock | {{ cols }} | Overall | Reason |
Stocks: [your stocks to watch]
It asks for scores against stated criteria, never which stock to buy.
What you need: an AI service that can search the web (most paid plans can). The app doesn't connect to it and never sees your account.
Not tested: there's no backtest. Results start on the day the strategy starts, and depend on which AI you run it in.
What can go wrong: your AI can misread the news, or make things up, and a top-scored stock can still fall.
