Dip in winners

"Find the next Nvidia"

The idea

Pick winning stocks: companies whose price has climbed steadily for months because the market believes in them. Then wait for a short pullback, when the price dips a few percent.

IT SIGNALS WHEN

01
The long trend is up: the stock is above its SMA 200 (average price over the last 200 days).
02
Wait for a discount: it drops into the discount zone, 5-15% below its recent peak.
03
The dip is not a collapse: it is still above its SMA 50 (average price over the last 50 days).
