Ride the long trend

"Take the wins, dodge the losses"

The idea

Markets move in long waves. The strategy signals a buy of a broad index fund when the market moves into an uptrend, and a sale when the trend turns down, to dodge the dip. After the dip it looks for a new buy signal.

It signals when

BUY
The market closes more than 3% above its SMA 200 (average price over the last 200 days).
SELL
The market closes more than 3% below its SMA 200.
ALL
All in, all out: each signal covers the whole budget at once.
